# The Rive side

Everything 2D is Rive: RML generated by `make_rml.py` (as the kart's
`make_rml.py` does, with the Hedra samples' RML library,
`rive_modeler/samples/ui/rml_ui.py`, for its builders: the document, view
models, machines, listeners, focus), baked with the world into one
`.riv`. The samples' design system (its frosted glass, Inter and
Iosevka, its hairlines) is **not** this game's look: Avalanche Air uses
the library's builders and its own tokens, the 1998 ones (LOOK.md,
section 8).

## 1. Artboards

| Artboard | What |
|---|---|
| `Main` | the root: the `Scene3D` (full window, always present: the title's attract, the run, the results' backdrop) and over it a `Solo` of the overlays, the wipe, the captions |
| `TitleOverlay` | "PRESS START" (blinking), the scheme picker, the high score table (cycling with the attract), the credits line |
| `HudOverlay` | score, combo multiplier, timer, the trick banner, the praise word, the combo's running sum, the balance meter, the countdown, the grade targets, the progress strip, the perfect flash |
| `ResultsOverlay` | the tally, the total, the grade stamp, the best combo, the prompts |
| `InitialsOverlay` | the three letters and the table with the new entry |
| `PauseOverlay` | Resume, Restart, Volume, Controls, Ghost, Assists, Quit to title |
| `Wipe` | the snow wipe covering a respawn (RIDER.md, section 9) |
| `Captions` | one line at the bottom for the announcer's lines that are not on screen |
| components | `TrickBanner`, `Praise`, `ComboMeter`, `BalanceMeter`, `Countdown`, `GradeStamp`, `TallyRow`, `ScoreRow`, `Glyph` (a button shown as the pad's or the keyboard's), `MenuItem` |

The Scene3D is the hedra_engine library's scripted layout (`hedra build`
vendors it), drawing `world/run.rscn`: the PSX frame rig with the course,
the rider and the cameras (LOOK.md, section 1).

## 2. The view model `Game`

### Written by the menus and the HUD (read by the world)

| Property | Kind | What |
|---|---|---|

| `scheme` | number | 0 Hot Pink, 1 Acid, 2 Blaze |
| `line` | number | unused by the player (the line is ridden, not chosen); the autopilot's choice in demo and tests |
| `settings/musicVolume`, `sfxVolume`, `voiceVolume` | number 0 to 1 | |

| `pad/x`, `pad/y`, `pad/buttons` | number | the pad's left stick and a button bitmask, written by the 2D pad script until E-PAD (section 4) |
| `demo`, `audioBank` | boolean | the demo mode; the degraded audio tier's render (AUDIO.md, section 10) |

### Written by the world (read by the HUD)

| Property | Kind | What |

## 3. State machines

### `Main` (on `Main`)

| Layer | States | Moves on |
|---|---|---|
| Screen | `Title`, `Run`, `Results`, `Initials` | `phase` |
| Pause | `Off`, `On` (the Scene3D's `playing` false, `PauseOverlay` shown, focus in its menu) | Escape, Select, Resume |
| Wipe | `Idle`, `Wipe` (0.15 s in, 0.15 s out) | `crashSeq` after 1.0 s, unless reduced motion |
| Captions | `Hidden`, `Line` (3 s) | `captionSeq`, when `captions` |
| Contrast | `Normal`, `High` | `highContrast` |
| Focus | which overlay holds the keys: the Scene3D during the run (so keys reach the world), the overlay's menu otherwise | the screen |

Start / R in `Run` or `Results`: the listener adds one to `restartSeq`;
in `Title`: `startSeq`. Escape and Select toggle Pause. The restart's
press to the rider's control is the world's (RIDER.md, section 9).

### `Hud` (on `HudOverlay`)

| Layer | What |
|---|---|
| Banner | `Idle` → `Live` (the growing name, small, at the banner's place) → `Slam` on `bankSeq` (160 % → 92 % → 100 % in 0.25 s, a 1-pixel shake) → `Hold` (1.2 s) → `Out` (slides left 0.15 s); reduced motion: a fade |
| Praise | on `bankSeq` with `praise` > 0: the word slams 0.1 s after the banner, holds 1.0 s |
| Perfect | `perfectSeq`: a 120 ms border flash at 30 % (none with reduced motion) and "PERFECT!" above the banner |
| Combo | `combo/active`: the running sum and "x N" under the banner, the multiplier's digit bouncing on each new trick |
| Balance | `grinding`: the meter under the rider (at `riderX`, `riderY` + 20 px), its needle at `balance`, red past 0.7 |
| Countdown | `countdown`: big chrome digits, each slamming on its beat; "GO!" in acid green; the grade targets line during the count |
| Timer | the clock, always during the run |
| Crash | `crashSeq`: "OUCH!" or "WIPEOUT!" as the praise word |

### `Results` (on `ResultsOverlay`)

The lines appear one by one (0.35 s apart), each counting up as the world
writes it (the tally ticks on the music's 16ths, AUDIO.md, section 8),
then the total, then after 0.5 s the grade stamped (`GradeStamp`: 300 %
→ 100 % in 0.18 s, a 2-pixel shake, dust), then the best combo's text,
then the prompts ("START: RIDE AGAIN", "SELECT: MENU": the pause menu, whose last item is Quit to title). A score in the
table goes to `Initials` after the stamp.

## 4. The pins: how the world reads and writes the view model

Node scripts cannot reach the view model today (E-VM), so the world uses
the kart's pins (its RIVE.md, section 4): hidden `Node3D`s under `Pins`
whose `position:x` carries a number, bound `to_source` (world → view
model) or `one_way` (view model → world); booleans ride a node's
`visible`. A pin carries no string, so the strings (`trick/text`,
`captionText`, the table's initials) are composed on the Rive side from
numbers the world pins:

- `trick/text` is built by a small 2D script on `HudOverlay`
  (`ui/trick_text.as`) from numbers the world pins: `trick/spin` (0 to
  1440), `trick/dir` (0 none, 1 frontside, 2 backside), `trick/flip` (0,
  1 front, 2 back, 3 double front, 4 double back), `trick/grab1`,
  `trick/grab2` (0 to 4), `trick/switch`, `trick/grind` (0 to 5) and
  `trick/live`;
- `captionText` likewise from `captionLine` (a line id);
- the table's initials as three numbers each (letter codes), joined by the
  same script.

When E-VM lands, `run.as` writes the view model directly and the pins and
these scripts go. Counts like the kart's: about 40 numbers out, 15 in.

**The pad until E-PAD**: `ui/pad.as`, a 2D `Node` script on `Main`,
overrides `gamepadEvent(e)`: it writes `pad/x`, `pad/y` (the left stick
with its dead zone) and `pad/buttons` (south, east, west, north, start,
select, d-pad as bits), and handles the menus' focus directly. The world
reads the three through inbound pins: one frame of latency, acceptable
for a stick, noticeable for an ollie's timing (8 ms at 120 Hz ticks: the
lab measures it). With E-PAD, the `Scene3D` forwards the events to
`input.as` and the pins go.

## 5. The HUD

Laid out on the 320 x 240 grid (section 7), every number white with a
1-pixel black outline and a hard 1-pixel shadow, the labels in the
pixel font, the trick names in the display face.

| Element | Place (grid pixels) | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Score | top left, (8, 8) | "SCORE" small, the score below it, 6 digits |
| Combo multiplier | top right, right-aligned at (312, 8) | "x5" big in pink while a combo is active; dim "x1" otherwise |
| Timer | top centre, (160, 8) | "1:23.45" |
| Trick banner | centre, y 70 to 100 | the name (display face, 14 to 18 px tall), its points under it |
| Praise | centre, y 104 to 124 | "SICK!" |
| Combo sum | under the praise, y 128 | "2,450 x 3" while active |
| Balance meter | under the rider | a 48 x 6 bar with a needle |
| Progress strip | bottom, y 228, 200 px wide, centred | the course's seven sections as segments, a dot for the rider |
| Countdown | centre | 3, 2, 1, GO, 48 px chrome digits |
| Grade targets | under the countdown | "S 50,000 · A 35,000 · B 20,000" |

It never covers the middle third of the picture's height around the
rider for more than the banner's 1.2 s.

## 6. The screens

**Title.** The chrome logo spins in 3D (a model in the world, in
`psx_chrome`, seen by the title camera) over the attract mode: the best
recorded run of the session (or the reference run on first launch)
replayed from its inputs, cut between the attract cameras every 4 to 6 s
(SPEC.md, section 7). "PRESS START" blinks with the music's beat. Left
and right change the scheme: the attract cuts to the `Gate` camera for
2 s, where the rider stands in the start gate wearing it.
The high score table replaces the logo for 6 s every 20 s.

**Run.** The HUD over the 3D; nothing else.

**Results.** The 3D behind it: the finish camera, its depth of field
blurring the arch and the crowd (LOOK.md, section 7). The tally,
the stamp, the prompts (section 3).

**Initials.** Three big letters, the cursor's under a blinking caret;
up and down change it, left and right move, south / Enter confirms; typed
letters fill it; then the table with the new row flashing, then back to
the results' prompts.

**Pause.** A slanted panel, its items in the display face; the volumes
as 10-step bars; Controls shows the mapping picture of the last device
used.

## 7. Layout: the 320 x 240 grid

- The overlays are 320 x 240 artboards (426 x 240 in widescreen), drawn
  scaled by the largest whole number that fits the window, centred, the
  rest black (the 3D's pillarbox is the same, LOOK.md, section 1). A
  2D layout script (`ui/fit.as`, as the kart's) computes the scale from
  the window and writes it.
- Text and shapes are placed on whole grid pixels; motion snaps to them
  (a slam's scale steps are chosen so edges land on pixels).
- The fonts: a pixel font at its design size or a whole multiple, a
  display face at sizes whose strokes are whole pixels; both SIL OFL,
  chosen at A0 (ASSETS.md, section 6).
- Images (the logo's 2D form, the grade letters, the glyphs) are drawn at
  their 1x pixel size times the scale with a nearest sampler.

## 8. Demo mode

`Game/demo` true (`--data=data/demo=true`): every overlay hidden, input
ignored, volume 0. The world plays the reference run's recording (its
inputs from seed 1) through the kicker section and the rail garden, seen
by the attract cameras: 0 to 6 s `Attract2` (a crane over K3 as the rider
spins a 720 Indy), 6 to 12 s `Attract3` (a low dolly beside K5's
backflip), 12 to 20 s `Attract4` (over the rail garden, the combo through
the centre lane), 20 to 26 s `Attract5` (the frozen lake in the
foreground, its reflection), 26 to 30 s `Attract2` again on a matching
moment of K3 for the seam. The loop's 30 s, its seam and its checks are
the shared rules (`rive_modeler/docs/plans/SHOWCASE.md`, "Demo mode").

## 9. Accessibility in Rive

Every number is a text run bound through converters, so `rive
--semantics` reads the HUD; the menus are focusable and answer the arrows
and Enter (Rive's focus) and the pad (through `ui/pad.as`); reduced motion
replaces slams and shakes with fades; captions and high contrast as in
SPEC.md, section 12.
